# Changelog

Every edition of Hargreaves, newest first. Each section here becomes the notes on its GitHub release.

## [1.0.1] - 2026-11-08

My sincere apologies. In my first edition I mislaid my bow tie, and without it I declined to leave the hall: the app would not get past its opening screen.

### Put right

- **The app opens again.** The bow tie (the notification icon) was being tidied away when the app was built for release. It now stays.
- **I shall never again keep you waiting at the door.** Should Android refuse me anything while I set up my reminders, I carry on rather than standing frozen.

If you installed 1.0.0, please uninstall it and install 1.0.1. From this edition onwards I shall fetch new editions myself, from **Instructions › New editions**.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-08

Good day. Hargreaves, at your service, in his first edition.

### What I shall do for you

- **A sensible daily amount**, worked out from your weight, age, exertion and climate, and yours to correct.
- **Glasses at the proper hours**, spread from the moment you rise until an hour before you retire.
- **Two manners of reminder:** a discreet note, or, should you prefer, in full over the locked screen ("At the door").
- **Answer from the reminder itself:** *Thank you* records the glass without opening the app; *In 15 minutes* and *Not now* are there too.
- **A kind word and a curiosity** with every glass, all carried in the app.
- **Today:** your glasses at a glance, a different amount when you take one, and an undo for the slips.
- **The Cellar Book:** week and month at a glance, honest remarks, never a scolding.
- **Standing Instructions:** your hours, glass size, chimes, whether I ask again if ignored, and help with an overzealous battery warden.
- **New editions:** I look once a day and fetch a new one when you say so, checking it before Android installs it.

### On your privacy

No account. Nothing about you leaves the telephone. My only errand outside is asking GitHub whether a new edition exists.
